Ethics
Story and Representation
The film is based on my real childhood memories and my relationship with my father.

The events described in the narration are personal memories. However, the visual characters generated for the film will be fictionalized representations rather than attempts to reproduce the exact appearance of my father or my childhood appearance.

Likeness and Consent
The film will not use an AI-generated replica of my father's face or voice.

The father and daughter shown in the generated visuals will be representative fictional characters based on descriptions in storyboard.json.

This decision reduces the risk of creating a misleading synthetic representation of a real person.

Narration
The narration is based on my own story and is written in Amharic.

The narration voice is generated using Gemini text-to-speech rather than cloning the voice of myself, my father, or another identifiable person.

AI Disclosure
The film explicitly discloses the use of generative AI.

The final storyboard contains a dedicated AI disclosure end card with Amharic narration and English on-screen text.

English disclosure:

"This film was created with the assistance of generative AI."

The disclosure is included as part of the film pipeline rather than being added manually after production.

Human Review
AI-generated outputs are reviewed by me before being accepted.

For example, the Amharic narration was reviewed for pronunciation, natural phrasing, emphasis and pacing. Some AI-generated narration was regenerated or revised based on human evaluation.

A usable narration output was retained in one case when repeated attempts to improve a minor pronunciation issue failed, and this limitation is documented in FAILURES.md.

Models and Tools
The following tools are currently part of the production pipeline:

Gemini TTS: Amharic narration
FFmpeg: audio/video assembly and media processing
FFprobe: media-duration inspection
Image-generation model: to be documented once selected
Image-to-video model: to be documented once selected
Music-generation model: to be documented once selected
The specific model names, licences and output-use conditions will be added as each production stage is implemented.

Labelling Plan
AI use will be disclosed:

inside the film through the final disclosure card;
in the public project/repository documentation; and
in the accompanying public post or submission materials where applicable.
